## Part A: Listening Comprehension Battery

### Task A1: Spoken Comprehension & Speech Decoding

1. **Audio Clip 1 (Everyday Spoken English — Connected Speech & Reductions)**
   - **Spoken Text**: *"I was gonna get a cup of water, but the machine is out of order."*
   - **IPA Transcription**: `/aɪ wəz ˈɡənə ɡɛt ə kʌp əv ˈwɑːɾɚ | bət ðə məˈʃiːn ɪz ˌaʊt əv ˈɔːrdɚ/`
   - **Target Acoustic Features**:
     - Reduction of *going to* $\rightarrow$ [ˈɡənə]
     - Intervocalic alveolar flapping in *water* $\rightarrow$ [ˈwɑːɾɚ]
     - Preposition reduction of *of* $\rightarrow$ [əv] / [ə]
     - Consonant linking in *out of order* $\rightarrow$ [ˌaʊtəˈvɔːrdɚ]

2. **Audio Clip 2 (General Workplace Schedule — Numbers & Days)**
   - **Spoken Text**: *"We’re meeting on Tuesday morning at ten, not Thursday."*
   - **IPA Transcription**: `/wɪr ˈmiːtɪŋ ɑːn ˈtuːzdeɪ ˈmɔːrnɪŋ ət ˈtɛn | nɑːt ˈθɜːrzdeɪ/`
   - **Target Acoustic Features**:
     - Phonemic contrast between *Tuesday* /ˈtuːzdeɪ/ and *Thursday* /ˈθɜːrzdeɪ/
     - Weak preposition *at* $\rightarrow$ [ət]
     - Nuclear contrastive stress on *Tuesday* and *ten*

3. **Audio Clip 3 (Workplace Problem Report — Technical Contextual Probe)**
   - **Spoken Text**: *"The deployment failed because the database credentials expired at midnight."*
   - **IPA Transcription**: `/ðə dɪˈplɔɪmənt feɪld bɪˈkəz ðə ˈdeɪtəbeɪs krəˈdɛnʃəlz ɪkˈspaɪɚd ət ˈmɪdnaɪt/`
   - **Target Acoustic Features**:
     - Past tense marker /-d/ in *failed* and *expired*
     - Technical workplace compound *database credentials*
     - Syllabic stress on *deployment* [dɪˈplɔɪmənt] and *credentials* [krəˈdɛnʃəlz]

### Task A2: Micro-Dictation / Bottom-Up Decoding

1. **Sentence 1**:
   - **Spoken Text**: *"Can you send me the file?"*
   - **IPA Transcription**: `/kən jə ˈsɛnd miː ðə faɪl/`
   - **Connected Features**: Weak form *can* $\rightarrow$ [kən], weak form *you* $\rightarrow$ [jə], liquid coda in *file* [faɪl].

2. **Sentence 2**:
   - **Spoken Text**: *"What did you do yesterday?"*
   - **IPA Transcription**: `/ˈwʌdʒə ˈduː ˈjɛstɚdeɪ/`
   - **Connected Features**: Palatalization assimilation: *What did you* $\rightarrow$ [ˈwʌdʒə] or [ˈwʌdɪdʒə], flapped/elided auxiliary.

3. **Sentence 3**:
   - **Spoken Text**: *"I've been working on this bug."*
   - **IPA Transcription**: `/aɪv bɪn ˈwɜːrkɪŋ ɑːn ðɪs bʌɡ/`
   - **Connected Features**: Contracted auxiliary *I've been* [aɪv bɪn], preposition linking *on this* [ɑːn ðɪs], final voiced stop /ɡ/ in *bug*.

4. **Sentence 4**:
   - **Spoken Text**: *"There’s a problem with the server."*
   - **IPA Transcription**: `/ðɛrz ə ˈprɑːbləm wɪð ðə ˈsɜːrvɚ/`
   - **Connected Features**: Contraction *There's a* [ðɛrz ə], interdental fricative /ð/ in *with the*, syllabic /r/ in *server*.

### Optional Task A3: Advanced Acoustic Parsing Extension
*(For learners routing beyond intermediate level)*

- **Spoken Text**: *"Look, had we known the upstream broker was throttling the event stream, we wouldn't have spun up redundant consumer instances in the first place."*
- **IPA Transcription**: `/lʊk | həd wiː noʊn ðiː ˈʌpstriːm ˈbroʊkɚ wəz ˈθrɑːtlɪŋ ðiː ɪˈvɛnt striːm | wiː ˈwʊdənt həv spʌn ʌp rɪˈdʌndənt kənˈsuːmɚ ˈɪnstənsəz ɪn ðə fɜːrst pleɪs/`
- **Connected Features**: Inversion in third conditional (*had we known*), rapid modal contraction (*wouldn't have* $\rightarrow$ [ˈwʊdənt əv]), technical terms in fast connected stream (*upstream broker*, *event stream*, *redundant consumer instances*).
