- **Instrument Type**: Formative Diagnostic Battery (Learner-Facing Form)

- **Equipment Needed**: Computer or phone with headphones/earbuds and a voice recorder (e.g., Voice Memos on Mac/iPhone or Voice Recorder on Galaxy S24 Ultra).
- **Important Instructions**:
  - Do NOT use dictionaries, translators, AI assistants, or subtitles.
  - If you do not know an answer, leave it blank or write "I don't know". Guessing distorts your diagnostic profile.

## Part B: Reading Comprehension Battery (10 minutes)

### Task B1: General Workplace & Daily Narrative (Core Passage)
*Read the passage below and answer the 3 questions:*

> "Alex arrived at the office at 8:30 AM. His team had planned to release the new software update before noon. By 11:00 AM, they discovered an error in the data pipeline, fixed the bug together, and safely deployed the update at 1:30 PM."

1. **Item 1 (Detail Recall)**: When was the software update originally scheduled to be released?
