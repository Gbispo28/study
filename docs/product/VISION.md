# Product Vision: English Learning OS

## 1. Executive Summary

**English Learning OS** is an agentic, scientifically grounded personal operating system designed to guide a Portuguese-speaking technology professional from absolute beginner (CEFR pre-A1/A1) to professional English fluency (CEFR B2/C1).

Unlike static courses, fragmented mobile apps, or arbitrary flashcard decks, English Learning OS acts as an adaptive learning engine that coordinates study routines, active recall, sentence mining, conversational practice, and progress analytics within a strictly enforced daily 60-minute timebox.

---

## 2. Target User Persona

- **Background**: Brazilian software and data processing engineer.
- **Current Proficiency**: Starting English practically from zero (CEFR A0/A1).
- **Time Availability**: Exactly **1 focused hour per day** (must maximize return on time invested).
- **Hardware Ecosystem**:
  - **MacBook Air M4**: Primary environment for deep study, long-form reading, writing composition, code-assisted analysis, and desktop automation.
  - **Galaxy S24 Ultra**: Mobile device for on-the-go spaced repetition review (AnkiDroid), quick listening drills, voice practice, and micro-sessions.
- **AI Tooling Available**: Google AI Pro (Gemini 1.5/2.0 Pro/Flash), Claude Code Pro, Google Antigravity 2.0.
- **Key Pain Points**:
  - Significant difficulty memorizing isolated vocabulary through rote memorization.
  - High susceptibility to cognitive overload if study routines lack structure.
  - Frustration with courses that prioritize passive listening over active production.
- **Key Desires**:
  - Scientifically validated learning methods (evidence over gimmicks).
  - Measurable, auditable progress tracking.
  - Automation of tedious tasks (e.g., card generation, sentence mining, phonetic transcription).
  - Balanced mastery of **Speaking, Listening, Reading, Writing, Pronunciation, and Grammar**.
  - A sustainable, compounding habit that can endure for months and years.

### 3.1 Learning Science & Cognitive Foundation
Every pedagogical interaction is grounded in cognitive science:
- **Active Recall & Retrieval Practice**: Never passive recognition; the brain must be forced to reconstruct linguistic representations.
- **Modern Spaced Repetition (FSRS)**: Continuous memory stability tracking rather than fixed intervals.
- **Comprehensible Input ($i+1$)**: Exposing the learner to authentic language just slightly above their current competency, supported by scaffolded glosses.
- **Phonetic Grounding**: Early articulatory training to distinguish English phonemes not present in Brazilian Portuguese (e.g., vowel distinctions /i/ vs /ɪ/, /æ/ vs /ɛ/, dental fricatives /θ/ and /ð/).

### 3.2 The 60-Minute Daily Routine Engine
A sustainable, structured daily ritual optimized for maximum cognitive retention:
- **Review & Retrieval (15-20 min)**: Anki/FSRS review of prioritized sentence cards with audio.
- **Core Skill / Focused Input (25-30 min)**: Scaffolded reading, structured listening with transcript comparison, or targeted grammar in context.
- **Active Production & Output (10-15 min)**: Speaking aloud, pronunciation drills, or short written reflections evaluated by AI tutors.

### 3.3 Multi-Device & Agentic Automation
- Seamless interoperability between desktop (MacBook Air M4) and mobile (Galaxy S24 Ultra).
- Leveraging AI models (Gemini Pro, Claude) for sentence mining, grammar explanation, natural audio synthesis, and conversational simulation.
